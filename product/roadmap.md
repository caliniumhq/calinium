# Calinium Roadmap

Status: Living Document

Owner: Founder

Last Updated: 2026-07-21

---

# Purpose

This roadmap defines the long-term evolution of Calinium.

It is not a list of engineering tasks.

Instead, it describes the major milestones that move Calinium closer to its vision.

Specific implementation details belong in project management tools.

---

# Phase 1 — Foundation

Status: In Progress

Objective:

Build the foundation for a premium Shopify storefront generation platform.

Focus:

- Product philosophy
- Design principles
- AI architecture
- Theme engine
- Documentation
- Internal tooling

Success means:

Calinium has a reliable foundation for future development.

---

# Phase 2 — Generation Engine

Objective:

Enable Calinium to transform business understanding into complete Shopify storefronts.

Focus:

- Merchant conversation
- Business understanding
- Brand analysis
- Store strategy
- Theme generation
- Live previews

Success means:

A merchant can describe a business and receive a professionally designed storefront.

---

# Phase 3 — Creative Intelligence

Objective:

Improve the quality of recommendations through deeper design reasoning.

Focus:

- Better visual recommendations
- Homepage strategy
- Navigation planning
- Collection strategy
- Conversion optimization
- Brand consistency

Success means:

Calinium begins making decisions like an experienced creative director.

---

# Phase 4 — Merchant Experience

Objective:

Create the simplest storefront creation experience available.

Focus:

- Guided conversation
- Faster onboarding
- Intelligent defaults
- Better recommendations
- Revision workflow
- Collaborative approvals

Success means:

Creating a storefront feels effortless.

---

# Phase 5 — Continuous Improvement

Objective:

Help merchants improve their storefront after launch.

Focus:

- Store analysis
- Design audits
- Performance insights
- Accessibility recommendations
- UX improvements
- Conversion suggestions

Success means:

Calinium becomes an ongoing creative partner instead of a one-time generator.

---

# Phase 6 — Brand Intelligence

Objective:

Understand businesses beyond their storefront.

Focus:

- Brand identity
- Visual consistency
- Messaging
- Content strategy
- Product storytelling
- Creative direction

Success means:

Calinium understands brands, not just Shopify stores.

---

# Phase 7 — Ecommerce Creative Platform

Objective:

Expand beyond storefront generation.

Possible capabilities include:

- Landing pages
- Seasonal campaigns
- Marketing assets
- Product launches
- Editorial content
- Creative planning

Success means:

Merchants use Calinium for more than storefront design.

---

# Phase 8 — Ecosystem

Objective:

Build an ecosystem around Calinium.

Potential areas:

- Integrations
- Extensions
- Marketplace
- Community
- Creative resources
- Educational content

Success means:

Calinium becomes a platform, not just a product.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Calinium should become the first place merchants go when they need creative work for their business.

Not because it replaces creativity.

Because it makes professional creativity accessible.

---

# Success Metrics

We know we're succeeding when merchants say:

"I didn't design my storefront."

"I described my business."

"Calinium handled the rest."

---

# Guiding Principle

Every new feature should answer one question:

Does this make creating a professional storefront simpler?

If the answer is no...

It doesn't belong in Calinium.

---

# North Star

The future of ecommerce is not learning how to design.

The future is explaining your business.

Calinium transforms that understanding into exceptional storefronts.
