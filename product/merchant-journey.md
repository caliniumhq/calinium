# Merchant Journey

Version: 1.0

---

# Vision

Calinium is not a website builder.

Calinium is an AI Creative Director that designs and builds Shopify storefronts uniquely tailored to each merchant.

The merchant should never feel like they are configuring software.

Instead, they should feel like they are collaborating with an experienced creative director who understands their business and makes expert recommendations.

Every interaction should reinforce one feeling:

> "Calinium understands my business."

---

# Core Philosophy

Merchants describe their business.

Calinium designs the storefront.

Merchants approve recommendations.

Calinium handles complexity.

The interface should remain simple enough that someone with no design or technical experience can complete the journey confidently.

Whenever possible:

- Calinium recommends.
- Merchants approve.

Never ask the merchant to make a decision that Calinium can determine with confidence.

---

# Merchant Journey

The complete experience consists of ten stages.

---

# Stage 1 — Homepage

The merchant visits:

calinium.com

The homepage has one primary action.

## Primary CTA

Start Designing

The page briefly explains:

- Describe your business.
- Calinium understands your goals.
- A Shopify storefront is designed specifically for your brand.

Below the primary CTA, a secondary link is available.

Need something ready today?

Browse Premium Themes

This option is intentionally secondary.

The primary experience is creating a unique storefront.

---

# Stage 2 — Conversation Begins

After clicking Start Designing, the merchant enters a distraction-free conversation.

There are no forms.

There are no dashboards.

There are no technical settings.

The AI introduces itself.

Calinium:

Hi, I'm Calinium.

I'll help you design a Shopify storefront tailored specifically to your business.

Let's start with one simple question.

What do you sell?

---

# Stage 3 — Understanding the Business

The merchant answers naturally.

Example:

Merchant:

I sell handmade leather travel bags.

Calinium responds naturally.

Great.

Who usually buys your bags?

The conversation continues naturally.

The AI quietly extracts structured information behind the scenes.

The merchant never sees schemas or technical fields.

As confidence increases, Calinium begins confirming its understanding.

Example:

Here's what I understand so far.

Business:
Premium handmade leather travel bags

Audience:
Frequent travelers

Positioning:
Luxury

Is that correct?

The merchant can:

- Confirm
- Edit
- Continue explaining

Only confirmed information becomes part of the Brand Blueprint.

---

# Stage 4 — Brand Assets

As soon as the AI discovers that assets exist, it requests them naturally.

Example:

Perfect.

Do you already have a logo?

If yes:

Great.

Upload it now and I'll use it to understand your brand.

Additional uploads appear only when relevant.

Examples include:

- Product photography
- Lifestyle photography
- Brand guidelines
- Campaign images
- Videos

Uploads are requested only when they improve the final storefront.

---

# Stage 5 — Brand Blueprint

Once enough information has been collected, Calinium prepares the Brand Blueprint.

This is not raw data.

It is presented like a creative agency brief.

Sections include:

- Business Summary
- Audience
- Brand Personality
- Goals
- Existing Assets
- Visual Direction
- Color System
- Typography Direction
- Inspirations
- Competitor Observations
- Recommendations

The merchant reviews the Blueprint.

Every section can be edited.

---

# Stage 6 — Store Strategy

After approval of the Brand Blueprint, Calinium begins creating a Store Strategy.

The merchant sees:

"I'm creating the best storefront strategy for your business."

This stage explains:

- Homepage direction
- Navigation philosophy
- Product page approach
- Collection experience
- Trust strategy
- Storytelling strategy
- Mobile experience
- Conversion philosophy

Calinium explains recommendations only when the merchant asks.

The interface remains clean.

---

# Stage 7 — Theme Preview

Calinium generates a preview of the storefront.

The preview should feel like opening a professionally designed Shopify theme.

The merchant can browse:

- Homepage
- Collections
- Product page
- Cart
- Mobile preview
- Menu
- Footer

The merchant may request revisions.

Examples:

"Make it feel more premium."

"Reduce the animations."

"I'd like warmer colors."

Calinium updates the preview accordingly.

---

# Stage 8 — Payment

Once the merchant approves the preview, payment is requested.

The merchant understands exactly what they are purchasing.

There are no surprises.

The merchant purchases:

A fully customized Shopify storefront designed specifically for their business.

---

# Stage 9 — Final Generation

After payment, Calinium performs the final production build.

The merchant sees live generation progress.

Rather than percentages, progress is described in meaningful milestones.

Examples:

✓ Understanding complete

✓ Strategy approved

✓ Theme generated

✓ Performance optimized

✓ Accessibility verified

✓ SEO prepared

Generating final package...

---

# Stage 10 — Delivery

Once generation completes, the merchant receives:

- Shopify Theme ZIP
- Theme documentation
- Installation guide

If Shopify is connected, the merchant can optionally deploy directly.

Otherwise:

Download Theme

The merchant owns a storefront built specifically for their business.

---

# Product Principle

At every stage, the merchant should feel:

"I never had to learn web design."

"I simply described my business."

"Calinium did the difficult part."

That feeling is more important than the number of AI features inside the product.

Whenever there is uncertainty about a feature, ask one question:

Would a world-class creative director figure this out?

If yes:

Calinium should figure it out.
