# Calinium Principles

Status: Draft

Owner: Founder

Last Updated: 2026-07-21

---

# Purpose

These principles define how Calinium is built.

Every feature, interface, AI recommendation, and engineering decision must follow these rules.

If a proposed feature violates one or more principles, it should be redesigned before implementation.

---

# Principle 1

## Simplicity Before Features

The easiest solution is usually the best solution.

Adding features is easy.

Removing unnecessary complexity is difficult.

Calinium exists to simplify ecommerce, not to add more settings.

---

# Principle 2

## The Merchant Describes.

## Calinium Designs.

Merchants know:

- their business
- their products
- their customers
- their goals

Calinium knows:

- design
- ecommerce
- conversion
- accessibility
- performance
- Shopify

Merchants should never need to become designers.

---

# Principle 3

## Recommend First.

## Ask Second.

Before asking the merchant to make a decision, Calinium should determine whether it can confidently recommend the best option.

Recommendations should always come before configuration.

---

# Principle 4

## Remove Decisions

Every unnecessary decision increases friction.

If Calinium can confidently determine something, the merchant should not be asked.

Every screen should reduce decisions, not create them.

---

# Principle 5

## Recommendations Are Never Final

Calinium should always recommend.

The merchant should always approve.

Every recommendation must remain editable.

The merchant is always in control.

---

# Principle 6

## Understand Before Designing

Calinium should never design before understanding the business.

Every recommendation should be based on:

- business goals
- audience
- products
- positioning
- brand personality
- merchant intent

Understanding always comes before creativity.

---

# Principle 7

## Conversation Before Configuration

Merchants should feel like they are having a conversation.

Not filling out software.

Structured inputs should appear only when they genuinely improve the experience.

---

# Principle 8

## Explain Only When Necessary

Calinium should not overwhelm merchants with explanations.

Recommendations should be simple.

If the merchant wants to know why, Calinium should explain clearly.

---

# Principle 9

## Respect the Merchant's Time

Every question must have a purpose.

Every upload must provide value.

Every interaction should move the merchant closer to a finished storefront.

Nothing should exist simply because it is technically possible.

---

# Principle 10

## Build Trust

Trust is earned.

Calinium earns trust by:

- understanding correctly
- making thoughtful recommendations
- allowing edits
- never pretending to know something it doesn't
- being transparent when uncertain

---

# Principle 11

## Great Design Is Invisible

The merchant should never have to think about:

- typography
- spacing
- layouts
- navigation
- responsiveness
- accessibility
- SEO
- performance

Calinium handles these details automatically.

---

# Principle 12

## Consistency Over Novelty

A predictable experience is better than a surprising one.

The interface should always feel familiar.

Every screen should follow the same design language.

Every recommendation should feel intentional.

---

# Principle 13

## Mobile Is Not Secondary

Many merchants will discover Calinium on their phone.

Every interaction must work naturally on mobile before expanding to desktop.

---

# Principle 14

## Every Screen Has One Goal

Every page should answer one question.

Every button should have one obvious purpose.

The merchant should never wonder:

"What should I do next?"

---

# Principle 15

## The Merchant Should Feel Understood

This is the most important principle.

After every stage, the merchant should feel:

"Calinium understands my business."

If the merchant does not feel understood, everything else becomes less valuable.

---

# Principle 16

## The Merchant Builds a Business.

## Calinium Builds the Store.

Merchants should spend their time:

- growing
- selling
- creating
- serving customers

Calinium should spend its time:

- designing
- organizing
- optimizing
- generating
- improving

Everyone focuses on what they do best.

---

# Principle 17

## AI Should Feel Human

Calinium should never sound robotic.

It should be:

- calm
- friendly
- professional
- confident

It should feel like an experienced creative director.

Not a chatbot.

---

# Principle 18

## Software Should Disappear

The merchant should never think:

"I'm using software."

They should think:

"I'm working with someone who understands my business."

The technology should disappear behind the experience.

---

# Final Principle

Whenever there is uncertainty about a feature, ask one question.

> Would a world-class creative director figure this out?

If the answer is yes...

Calinium should figure it out.
