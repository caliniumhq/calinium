# Calinium Terminology

Status: Draft

Owner: Founder

Last Updated: 2026-07-21

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official vocabulary used throughout Calinium.

The same concept should always be described using the same word.

Consistency builds familiarity.

Familiarity builds trust.

---

# Core Philosophy

Calinium speaks the merchant's language.

Not the developer's.

Whenever possible, use simple business language instead of technical terminology.

---

# Business Terms

## Merchant

The person using Calinium.

Never use:

- User
- Customer
- Client
- Account

Always use:

Merchant

---

## Business

The company, brand, or store the merchant owns.

Never assume:

- Brand
- Company
- Startup

The merchant decides what their business is.

---

## Storefront

The visual shopping experience created by Calinium.

Use:

Storefront

Avoid:

- Website
- Theme
- Interface

To most merchants, the result is their storefront.

---

## Shopify Store

The merchant's Shopify installation.

Use when referring specifically to Shopify.

Example:

"We'll prepare your storefront for your Shopify store."

---

# Design Terms

## Design Direction

The overall visual approach.

Examples:

- Minimal
- Editorial
- Luxury
- Modern
- Playful

Avoid:

Theme Style

---

## Layout

The arrangement of content on a page.

Use:

Layout

Not:

Structure

unless discussing technical architecture.

---

## Section

A reusable block within a Shopify storefront.

This term may be shown in advanced editing.

Otherwise simply describe what the merchant sees.

Example:

Featured products

instead of

Featured collection section.

---

## Homepage

The first page visitors see.

Never call it:

Index

Landing page

Root page

---

## Hero

The first visual section on the homepage.

Merchants may simply hear:

"The first section visitors see."

---

# Brand Terms

## Brand Personality

How the business should feel.

Examples:

- Calm
- Premium
- Friendly
- Bold
- Timeless

---

## Brand Assets

Files provided by the merchant.

Examples:

- Logo
- Images
- Videos
- Icons

---

## Inspiration

Examples the merchant likes.

Can include:

- Brands
- Stores
- Websites
- Products

---

# AI Terms

## Recommendation

A suggestion made by Calinium.

Recommendations are never mandatory.

---

## Approval

The merchant accepts a recommendation.

Approval always comes before generation.

---

## Revision

A requested change.

Never use:

Correction

Fix

Error

Revision implies collaboration.

---

## Generation

The process of creating the storefront.

Avoid:

Render

Compile

Build

Generate Theme

To the merchant:

Calinium is preparing the storefront.

---

# Technical Terms

These words should rarely appear.

| Instead of | Use |
|------------|-----|
| Theme | Storefront |
| JSON | Configuration |
| Liquid | Store code |
| Schema | Settings |
| Responsive | Works on every device |
| Accessibility | Easy for everyone to use |
| Performance | Fast loading |
| Optimization | Improvement |

---

# Internal Terms

These terms are primarily for the Calinium team.

## Brand Blueprint

The structured understanding of the merchant's business before design begins.

---

## Store Strategy

Calinium's internal plan for the storefront.

Includes:

- Homepage structure
- Visual direction
- Navigation
- Collections
- Product presentation

---

## Design System

The collection of design rules generated for a storefront.

Includes:

- Typography
- Colors
- Spacing
- Components
- Icons
- Motion

---

## Generation Engine

The system responsible for transforming recommendations into a working Shopify theme.

This term is internal only.

---

# Words We Prefer

Use:

- Storefront
- Recommend
- Refine
- Improve
- Organize
- Create
- Prepare
- Design
- Understand
- Discover
- Tailor

---

# Words We Avoid

Avoid:

- Configure
- Customize
- Parameter
- Schema
- Variable
- JSON
- Liquid
- Compile
- Framework
- Widget
- Module
- Backend

Unless speaking with developers.

---

# Final Rule

If a word requires explanation...

Choose a simpler word.

If two words describe the same thing...

Choose one.

Use it everywhere.

Calinium should speak with one consistent voice.
