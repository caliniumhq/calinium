# Calinium Preset System

## Purpose

The Calinium Preset System defines how complete storefront personalities are created, maintained, and generated across Calinium.

A preset is not merely a collection of colors or fonts.

It is a coherent design language that combines every part of the design system into a complete ecommerce experience.

Each preset should feel unique while remaining unmistakably Calinium.

Merchants should be able to launch a premium storefront by selecting the preset that best represents their brand—not by configuring hundreds of individual settings.

---

# Design Principles

## Principle 01 — Every Preset Has A Personality

Each preset should express a distinct visual identity.

Examples include:

- Editorial
- Luxury
- Minimal
- Contemporary
- Heritage
- Technical
- Organic

A preset should feel intentional rather than randomly customized.

---

## Principle 02 — Consistency Over Decoration

Changing a preset should never create inconsistency.

Every decision—from typography to spacing—should reinforce the same visual direction.

---

## Principle 03 — Curated Rather Than Unlimited

More choices do not necessarily create better designs.

Calinium favors carefully curated presets over unlimited customization.

Well-designed constraints produce better storefronts.

---

## Principle 04 — Products Remain The Hero

Regardless of personality, every preset should keep products as the primary visual focus.

Brand expression should support commerce—not distract from it.

---

## Principle 05 — Timelessness

Presets should remain relevant for years.

Avoid visual trends that may quickly become outdated.

Every preset should age gracefully.

---

# UX Foundations

## Hick's Law

A small number of high-quality presets reduces decision fatigue.

Merchants should quickly recognize the style that best matches their brand.

---

## Jakob's Law

Presets should preserve familiar ecommerce interactions.

Only visual expression changes.

Core usability remains consistent.

---

## Cognitive Load

Customers should never need to learn a new shopping experience because of a preset.

Brand identity may evolve.

Interaction patterns should remain predictable.

---

## Aesthetic–Usability Effect

Beautiful interfaces increase confidence.

Each preset should communicate quality through thoughtful design rather than excessive decoration.

---

# Preset Philosophy

A preset answers one question:

**"If this brand already existed, how would it naturally present itself online?"**

Presets should express identity—not novelty.

They should help merchants communicate who they are before a single word is read.

---

# Preset Structure

Every preset defines a complete visual language, including:

- Color system
- Typography
- Spacing
- Grid
- Corner radius
- Elevation
- Motion
- Components
- Iconography
- Imagery direction
- Section density
- Overall atmosphere

Changing a preset should update these systems together while preserving consistency.

# Merchant Experience

Merchants choose a preset based on their brand personality rather than individual visual settings.

The preset establishes a high-quality foundation while still allowing selective customization where appropriate.

---

# AI Guidelines

When generating storefronts, AI should determine the most appropriate preset by evaluating:

- Brand values
- Product category
- Target audience
- Price positioning
- Photography style
- Tone of voice
- Business goals

The selected preset should become the foundation for every subsequent design decision.

If no preset fully matches the merchant, AI may adapt an existing preset rather than invent an entirely new design language.

---

# Quality Checklist

Before approving a preset, ask:

- Does it communicate a clear personality?
- Does it remain unmistakably Calinium?
- Does it support product-first design?
- Does it preserve accessibility?
- Does it maintain performance?
- Does it feel timeless?
- Would merchants immediately recognize when to use it?

If not, continue refining the preset.

---

# Future Compatibility

New presets should expand the range of brand personalities without fragmenting the design system.

Every preset should inherit the same principles, accessibility standards, interaction patterns, and component behaviors.

Only the visual expression changes.

The underlying experience remains consistent.

The Calinium Preset System values identity, consistency, timelessness, and commerce-first design.

A preset should feel like a complete brand—not a different theme.
