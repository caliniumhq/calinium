# Footer

## Purpose

The Footer serves as the final destination of every page, providing essential navigation, business information, trust signals, and secondary actions.

It helps customers continue exploring the store, access important resources, and complete their journey with confidence.

The Footer should feel structured, calm, and unobtrusive.

---

## Responsibilities

The Footer is responsible for:

- providing secondary navigation
- displaying legal information
- presenting business details
- supporting customer service
- reinforcing trust
- collecting newsletter subscriptions (optional)
- remaining accessible across all devices

The Footer is not responsible for:

- displaying primary navigation
- replacing the Header
- presenting promotional campaigns
- competing for visual attention

These responsibilities belong to other components.

---

## User Goals

The Footer should help customers:

- locate important pages
- access customer support
- understand company information
- review legal policies
- continue browsing confidently

---

## Merchant Goals

The Footer should help merchants:

- improve site navigation
- reduce support requests
- increase customer trust
- improve SEO through structured navigation
- strengthen brand credibility

Merchants should configure content—not layout behavior.

---

## Structure

A Footer consists of:

- Footer Navigation — required
- Copyright — required

Optional:

- Logo
- Brand Description
- Newsletter
- Social Links
- Contact Information
- Payment Icons
- Country Selector
- Language Selector
- Trust Badges
- Legal Links

---

## Required Elements

Every Footer requires:

- footer navigation
- copyright notice
- accessible structure
- responsive layout

Customers should immediately understand where to find important information.

---

## Optional Elements

The Footer may include:

- company logo
- brand story
- newsletter signup
- social media links
- contact information
- payment methods
- store policies
- market selectors
- certifications

Optional content should remain secondary to navigation.

---

- navigation

- legal links

Recommended for most storefronts.

### Commerce

Displays:

- navigation

Suitable for feature-rich stores.

---

### Editorial

Emphasizes brand storytelling alongside navigation.

Recommended for luxury brands.

---

## Component-Specific Rules

### Navigation Rules

Footer navigation should include:

- customer service
- company
- policies

Optional:

- collections
- journal
- careers
- wholesale

Navigation should remain easy to scan.

---

### Branding Rules

If displayed, branding may include:

- logo
- short company description
- philosophy statement

Brand messaging should remain concise.

---

### Newsletter Rules

If included, the Newsletter component should follow its own specification.

The Footer should not duplicate newsletter messaging elsewhere on the page.

---

### Social Rules

Social links may include:

- Instagram
- Facebook
- Pinterest
- TikTok
- YouTube
- LinkedIn

Icons should include accessible labels.

---

### Contact Rules

Contact information may include:

- email
- phone
- business address
- operating hours

Information should remain accurate and easy to find.

---

### Legal Rules

Legal navigation may include:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Refund Policy
- Shipping Policy
- Cookie Policy
- Accessibility Statement

Legal links should remain available on every page.

---

### Payment Rules

Payment icons should:

- represent supported payment methods
- remain visually secondary
- avoid excessive emphasis

Icons should never imply unsupported payment methods.

---

## Supported States

### Default

The Footer displays normally.

---

### Loading

Optional dynamic content loads progressively.

Layout dimensions should remain stable.

---

### Expanded

Additional collapsible groups are visible.

Used primarily on mobile devices.

---

## Responsive Behaviour

The Footer should:

- adapt gracefully across all devices
- stack navigation logically
- preserve comfortable spacing
- avoid horizontal scrolling

Large navigation groups may collapse on smaller screens.

---

## Accessibility

Every Footer must support:

- semantic footer element
- proper heading hierarchy
- keyboard navigation
- visible focus indicators
- sufficient contrast
- accessible social links
- screen reader compatibility

Navigation groups should include descriptive headings.

---

## Shopify Settings

Merchants may configure:

- logo
- footer menu
- secondary menu
- brand description
- newsletter
- social links
- payment icons
- contact information
- country selector
- language selector
- color scheme

Merchants should not configure:

- typography scale
- spacing
- responsive breakpoints
- animation timing

These belong to the Design System.

---

## Design Tokens

The Footer should use semantic tokens for:

- spacing
- typography
- borders
- divider colors
- background colors
- transitions

Example token categories:

- footer-spacing
- footer-background
- footer-heading
- footer-navigation
- footer-divider
- footer-transition

---

## Motion Rules

Motion should remain restrained.

Allowed motion:

- fade transition
- accordion expansion
- opacity transition

Avoid:

- bouncing
- scaling
- decorative animations

Motion should support navigation rather than attract attention.

---

## Performance Rules

The Footer should:

- minimize JavaScript
- lazy-load non-critical assets
- avoid layout shifts
- optimize icon delivery
- reuse shared navigation components

The Footer should remain lightweight regardless of content volume.

---

## AI Guidelines

When generating storefronts, AI should:

- organize navigation logically
- prioritize customer support links
- preserve accessibility
- reuse Newsletter and Navigation components
- maintain restrained visual hierarchy

AI should never invent business information, policies, contact details, payment methods, or social accounts.

---

## Quality Checklist

### Purpose

- Navigation is easy to understand.
- Important pages are easy to locate.

### Design

- Navigation hierarchy is clear.
- Branding remains secondary.
- Whitespace is generous.

### Accessibility

- Keyboard navigation functions correctly.
- Focus indicators remain visible.
- Social links include accessible labels.

### Responsive

- Layout adapts across devices.
- Navigation stacks naturally.
- No horizontal scrolling occurs.

### Performance

- Layout remains stable.
- Icons load efficiently.
- Navigation remains responsive.

### AI Compatibility

- Component structure is deterministic.
- Existing components are reused.
- Merchant content remains accurate.

---

## Future Compatibility

Before extending the Footer, ask:

- Does the addition improve customer navigation?
- Can an existing Footer pattern satisfy the requirement?
- Will merchants understand the configuration?
- Does it preserve accessibility?
- Can AI generate it consistently?

If an existing pattern satisfies the requirement, reuse it.

The Footer should evolve through refinement rather than expansion.

Every Footer should provide clear navigation, strengthen customer trust, preserve accessibility and performance, and conclude every page with the calm, timeless design philosophy that defines Calinium.
