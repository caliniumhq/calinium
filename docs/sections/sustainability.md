# Sustainability

**Purpose and use:** Presents only verified environmental or social initiatives, metrics, and certifications.

**Settings and blocks:** Heading/copy, verification note, CTA, and spacing. `initiative`, `metric`, and `certification` blocks support the relevant evidence fields.

**Assets and safety:** Every claim, percentage, certification, organization, and metric is merchant-only. AI cannot generate or publish sustainability claims without verified merchant input.

**Accessibility and performance:** Text accompanies all icon/logo information; semantic lists and responsive media remain useful without JavaScript.

**Composition and limitation:** Place after Materials or Process and before recognition/contact. One instance per template. Dependencies: feature-item, statistic, logo-item, heading, icon, button.
