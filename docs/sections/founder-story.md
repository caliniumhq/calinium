# Founder Story

**Purpose and use:** Introduce a real founder on an About page or mid-homepage brand story. Use portrait-left/right and contained/full-width layouts.

**Settings and blocks:** Portrait, mobile portrait, eyebrow, heading, biography, quote, signature, CTA, alignment, and layout. `achievement` blocks contain an icon, heading, and supporting text.

**Assets and safety:** Portraits and signatures are merchant-supplied. Biography, quotes, and achievements require merchant confirmation; AI must not invent a person, history, credential, or award.

**Accessibility and performance:** Semantic heading, blockquote, responsive lazy portrait, and list semantics. It is server-rendered with no JavaScript.

**Composition and limitation:** Use once, after an editorial hero or manifesto and before Values or Timeline. Omit it when a real founder story is unavailable. Dependencies: `section-heading`, `rich-text`, `button`, `responsive-image`, `icon`.
