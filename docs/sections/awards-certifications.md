# Awards & Certifications

**Purpose and use:** Displays merchant-verified awards, memberships, press recognition, standards, and certifications.

**Settings and blocks:** Heading group, responsive columns, and spacing. `award` and `certification` blocks contain logo or icon, title, issuer, year, detail, and optional verification link.

**Assets and safety:** Recognition, organization, date, logo, certification status, and verification link are merchant-only. Do not render this section without real supporting evidence.

**Accessibility and performance:** Text identifies logo-only entries; external verification links use visible labels. Responsive logos are lazy loaded and there is no JavaScript.

**Composition and limitation:** Use once after verified commitments/values and before a closing CTA. Dependencies: heading, responsive image, icon, button.
