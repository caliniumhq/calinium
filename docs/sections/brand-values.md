# Brand Values

**Purpose and use:** Communicates high-level principles or philosophy in grid, list, or editorial presentation.

**Settings and blocks:** Heading/copy, CTA, layout, featured first value, responsive columns, and ratio. Each `value` block supports icon, image, heading, and text.

**Assets and safety:** AI may suggest neutral categories, but policies, commitments, guarantees, labor practices, and donations require merchant confirmation.

**Accessibility and performance:** Semantic list, responsive media, decorative icons, and no JavaScript. The featured value is not indicated by color alone.

**Composition and limitation:** Use once after Founder Story or Manifesto and before Materials or Team. Dependencies: feature-item, heading, responsive image, icon, button.
