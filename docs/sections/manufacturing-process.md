# Manufacturing Process

**Purpose and use:** Shows a clear, numbered production or service workflow.

**Settings and blocks:** Heading group, desktop step count, color/spacing. Each `process_step` has icon, title, copy, optional image, duration, and statistic.

**Assets and safety:** Durations, statistics, facilities, testing, locations, and supply-chain details are merchant-verified facts; AI must not invent them.

**Accessibility and performance:** Uses an ordered list with visible numbered sequence and mobile stacking. It is server-rendered, responsive, and uses lazy optional media.

**Composition and limitation:** Use once after Materials or Craftsmanship and before Behind the Scenes or Sustainability. Dependencies: timeline-item, media-item, heading, icon.
