# Materials

**Purpose and use:** Explains merchant-confirmed materials, ingredients, fabrics, components, or resources for product education.

**Settings and blocks:** Heading group, grid/editorial layout, ratio, CTA, and spacing. Each `material` block supports image, icon, name, description, origin, and properties.

**Assets and safety:** Origins, properties, certifications, sustainability, purity, rarity, and quality are factual claims and need confirmation. Avoid environmental claims in defaults.

**Accessibility and performance:** Responsive lazy images, semantic list, decorative icons, and no JavaScript. Missing media does not remove text content.

**Composition and limitation:** Works before Craftsmanship, Manufacturing Process, or a product rail. Dependencies: heading, responsive image, rich text, icon, button.
