# Craftsmanship

**Purpose and use:** Explains verified artisan work, construction, or specialist knowledge alongside image or hosted video media.

**Settings and blocks:** Primary image/mobile image or hosted video, heading, copy, quote, CTA, media side, and ratio. `craft_step` blocks contain icon or image, title, and copy.

**Assets and safety:** Methods, handmade claims, locations, artisan counts, production times, and quotations require merchant confirmation. Video is muted and uses native controls.

**Accessibility and performance:** Semantic media, responsive images, keyboard-accessible native video controls, lazy media, and no required JavaScript.

**Composition and limitation:** Pair with Materials and follow with Process or Behind the Scenes. Dependencies: feature-item, heading, rich text, button, responsive image, icon.
