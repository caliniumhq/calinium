# Behind the Scenes

**Purpose and use:** Shows real workshop, studio, production, event, or team media in grid, editorial, or horizontal-scroll layouts.

**Settings and blocks:** Heading/copy, optional quote, layout, columns, and ratio. Each `gallery_item` provides image/mobile image or hosted video, caption, location, and date.

**Assets and safety:** Every image, video, caption, location, date, and contextual claim is merchant-only. AI cannot invent a setting, process, event, or person.

**Accessibility and performance:** Uses figure/figcaption and native video controls. Scroll mode progressively enhances native scrolling with controls and editor block reveal; grid/editorial modes remain server-rendered.

**Composition and limitation:** Best after Craftsmanship or Process and before newsletter/contact. One instance per template; media-heavy configurations have higher performance cost. Dependencies: heading, responsive image, icon, scroll carousel.
