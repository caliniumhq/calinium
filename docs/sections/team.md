# Team

**Purpose and use:** Introduces real team members, makers, advisors, or leaders; it is most suitable for About pages.

**Settings and blocks:** Heading group, responsive columns, image ratio, and spacing. `team_member` blocks contain portrait, name, role, biography, optional email, and optional link.

**Assets and safety:** All people fields, portraits, addresses, and links are merchant-only and must be confirmed. Avoid publishing private contact details without permission.

**Accessibility and performance:** Semantic list and articles, `mailto:` links only when present, responsive lazy portraits, and no JavaScript.

**Composition and limitation:** Usually follows Founder Story or Values and precedes contact. Use once. Dependencies: heading, responsive image, rich text.
